# Blog surfaces

Mode: Read for public pages, Operate for private publishing. The user delegated design and implementation; blog identity and supplied material are pending.

## Grounded directions

1. Library index: cool shelves of dated entries and quiet reading margins.
2. Travel notebook: chronological personal notes with compact route-like navigation.
3. Independent periodical: a prominent current article and measured archive.
4. Independent bookshop reading list: ink-blue masthead, plain reading ground, a dated index and marginal bookmarks.
5. Atlas index: categories as references, with the article as the main destination.
6. Calendar catalogue: a year-organized archive and chronological article list.
7. Record liner notes: brief editorial introductions beside long-form writing.

The assigned direction is 4. Hallmark's Vellum indigo colourway carries its printing-house material. The theme draw's music anatomy is replaced by an article index: there is no record, performance date, venue or film in the supplied material.

Challengers were judged against mixed-content reader recognition and clarity: emission-line rail, cutting-bench rail and transforming cape were declined because their equipment/control vocabulary makes ordinary reading less direct; provenance ribbon was declined because the blog has no custody or disputed-evidence data; grid specimen was declined because type construction is not the reading task; sampler was declined because there is no ordered curriculum. Kept disciplines: consistent status vocabulary, explicit chronology, adaptable navigation, typographic alignment and graceful empty states.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal reading index, led by writing rather than a promotional hero or repeated cards.

OWN-WORLD: Vellum indigo ground, ink-blue field, flat publication surfaces and measured rules. Display type frames the index; body type stays a workhorse with CJK fallbacks. Public controls are typographic; the writing interface uses the same tokens at a denser scale.

STORY: Readers see the latest published writing, narrow by category or search, then enter a calm article with an anchored chapter index. The owner moves between drafts, editor, preview and published articles.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A capped masthead with simple destinations, a short site introduction, one leading article and the start of the dated article index. Empty production content becomes a true empty state, not invented autobiography. The first reading action is an article title.

FORM: Independent bookshop reading list, grounded candidate 4; seed:5aa5d0fc94695615. Hallmark theme draw 54d0d1 is adapted to a blog's Index-first anatomy; there are no invented music assets or dates.
